# PharmaDali Pharmacy Admin — User Guide

A simple, step-by-step user manual for pharmacy owners, branch managers, and pharmacists.

---

## 1. Logging In (with Email Verification Code)

To keep customer health records and pharmacy sales secure, PharmaDali uses email verification every time you log in.

### Step-by-Step Login
1. **Open the Website**: Open your browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge) and go to your pharmacy's web link (e.g., `https://admin.pharmadali.com`).
2. **Enter Your Login Details**: Type in your registered **Email** and **Password**, then click **Log In**.
3. **Enter the 6-Digit Code**: 
   - Check your email inbox for a message from **PharmaDali Security**.
   - Copy or type the 6-digit verification code into the boxes on screen.
4. **Go to Your Workspace**: 
   - Once all 6 numbers are entered, the system verifies your code automatically.
   - **Store Managers & Admins** will be taken directly to the **Dashboard**.
   - **Pharmacists & Cashiers** will be taken directly to the **Point of Sale (POS)** counter.

> [!TIP]
> - **Didn't receive the email?** Check your Spam/Junk folder. After the 60-second timer reaches zero, click **"Resend Code"**.
> - **Code Expiry**: The code expires after 5 minutes.
> - **Too Many Wrong Attempts**: If you enter the wrong code 5 times, your screen will temporarily lock for security. Click **"Back to Login"** to start over.

## 2. Business Intelligence & Dashboard Overview

The **Dashboard** shows how your pharmacy is doing today at a single glance. It updates automatically whenever a transaction is completed.

### 2.1 Reading the Summary Cards
Along the top of the screen, you will see 5 color-coded cards:
- **Sales Today**: Total money collected today from counter sales and pickups.
- **Today's Transactions**: Total number of sales receipts and completed pickups made today.
- **Expiring Items**: Number of medicine batches that will expire within the next 30 days.  
  *(Turns Orange or Red when you have items expiring soon that need immediate attention).*
- **Low Stock Items**: Medicines running low that need to be reordered.
- **Expired Items**: Batches that have already passed their expiry date.  
  *(If Red, check your shelves and remove these medicines immediately).*

### 2.2 Checking Sales Trends
- In the **Sales Trend** box, click the dropdown in the corner to switch between **Daily**, **Weekly**, and **Monthly** views.
- Hover your mouse over any dot on the line graph to see the exact sales amount in pesos for that day.

### 2.3 Quick Insights & Inventory Health
- **Quick Insights**: See today's best-selling medicine, your most popular category (e.g., Antibiotics or Pain Relief), and your profit margin.
- **Inventory Health**: Displays medicines that are running out soon along with how long the remaining stock will last (e.g., *"Paracetamol — Will last less than 1 week"*). Click **Know More ➔** to jump directly to your stock list.

### 2.4 Using the Analytics Page
1. Click **Analytics** in the left menu.
2. Choose between:
   - **Demand**: Shows which medicines are selling the fastest and which ones are at risk of running out of stock.
   - **Sales**: Shows total income, discounts given, and profit.
3. **Smart AI Tips (Insights Box)**: Read the helpful tips provided on the left. The system looks at your recent sales and suggests what to restock before you run out.
4. Use the arrow buttons (`<` and `>`) to browse past weeks or months.

## 3. Point of Sale (POS) & Online Order Management

Use this module to ring up counter sales for walk-in customers and hand over pre-ordered medicines for pickup.

### 3.1 Selling Medicines to Walk-In Customers (POS)
1. **Find the Product**: In the **Point of Sale** screen, type the brand name or generic name into the search bar.
2. **Add to Order**: Click on the product from the list, type the quantity the customer is buying, and click **Add to Order**.
3. **Add Discounts (If Applicable)**:
   - If the customer is a **Senior Citizen** or **PWD**, select their discount from the dropdown (this applies the statutory 20% discount).
   - Type in the customer's **ID card number** in the box provided.
4. **Collect Payment**:
   - Choose how the customer is paying: **Cash**, **GCash**, **Maya**, or **Card**.
   - If paying with cash, type the amount handed by the customer into the **Cash Received** box. The system will automatically show how much change to give back.
5. **Complete the Sale**:
   - Click the blue **Complete Sale** button.
   - The receipt prints automatically from your receipt printer, and the system automatically deducts the items from the batch expiring the soonest.

---

### 3.2 Handling Customer Pickup Orders
Customers can order medicines through the PharmaDali Mobile App and pick them up at your store.

1. **Find the Order**: Click **Pickup Orders** in the left menu, then click the **Ready** tab. Click on the customer's name or order number.
2. **Check the Prescription & IDs**:
   - Look at the details panel on the right side of your screen.
   - **Doctor's Prescription**: If the order includes prescription medicines, click the prescription picture to open it. Check the doctor's name, license, and dosage instructions.
   - **Senior / PWD ID**: If a discount was requested, click the ID image to verify that the ID matches the customer.
   - **Payment Proof**: If already paid via GCash, verify their payment screenshot.
3. **Collect Payment & Hand Over Medicines**:
   - **If already paid online**: Check the medicine bag, hand it to the customer, and click **Complete Sale**.
   - **If paying at the counter**: Click **Enter Payment**, enter the cash or GCash reference, and click **Confirm Payment & Complete**.
4. The order is finished, and the customer will receive an alert on their phone that their pickup is complete.

## 4. Inventory Management

PharmaDali automatically keeps track of every batch of medicine and sells items with the nearest expiry date first so that stock does not go to waste.

### 4.1 Checking Stock & Priority Restocks
1. Click **Inventory** in the left menu.
2. Review the top numbers for total products, low stock items, and expiring medicines.
3. Use the search bar or category filters to find any medicine quickly.
4. Look at the **Priority Restocks** list on the right. These are medicines predicted to run out within 7 days.
5. Once you call your supplier to reorder an item, click **"Mark as Ordered"** so other staff members know it has already been ordered.

---

### 4.2 Adding a New Product to the System
1. Click the **+ Add Product** button in the top right corner.
2. Choose **Medicine** (for tablets, syrups, capsules) or **Non-Medicine** (for medical supplies or personal care).
3. Fill in the product details:
   - Generic name, brand name, category, dosage form (e.g., Capsule or Tablet), strength (e.g., 500mg), and selling price.
   - Turn on the **Prescription Required** switch if customers need a doctor's prescription to buy it.
4. Fill in the batch information from the delivery box:
   - Batch number, quantity received, expiration date (Month/Year), and supplier name.
5. Click **Save Product**. The item is now ready to be sold at the counter.

---

### 4.3 Adding New Stock to an Existing Product
When a fresh delivery of a medicine you already carry arrives:
1. Click on the product name in the Inventory list.
2. In the popup window, scroll down to the **Batches** section and click **+ Add Batch**.
3. Type in the new **Batch Number**, **Quantity Received**, **Expiry Date**, and **Supplier**.
4. Click **Save Batch**. The total stock count will update automatically.

---

### 4.4 Recording Damaged, Spoiled, or Expired Stock (Stock Out)
If a medicine bottle breaks, is damaged, or expires:
1. Click on the product in the Inventory list.
2. Click the yellow **Stock Out** button.
3. Enter the number of units that were damaged or spoiled.
   > [!NOTE]
   > The system will automatically remove the stock from the batch that is closest to expiring.
4. Click **Confirm Stock Out**.

---

### 4.5 Checking Stock History (Inventory Logs)
1. Click **Inventory Logs** in the left menu.
2. Here you can see a complete list of every stock change: sales made, new deliveries added, and damaged stock removed.
3. Use the filters to search by product name or date to see who made a change, when it was made, and why.
